HOW TO READ THE MAP

Shape carries meaning, it is not decoration. Objects are drawn at their true shape — a vector in Rn really is a column of n cells, an m×n matrix really is a grid m tall and n wide.

TRUE SHAPEan object: scalar, vector, matrix, tensor
CUBEan operation — something that does work
TALL CUBEa measurement — it turns objects into a number
FLAT PLATEstructure — a claim about the space itself
SOLID LINEthe learning path, in checklist order
THIN LINErelated, but not the next step

The tall structures are the measuring parts: magnitude, dot product, angle, distance and cosine similarity are exactly the operations that collapse vectors into a single number.
